Helix

Scientific data analysis, simplified.

Organize, analyze and visualize scientific data, from raw numbers to publication-ready graphs. Helix is a free, open-source and private alternative to GraphPad Prism for macOS.

Under the hood

Three engines. Everything runs on your Mac.

A fast data grid

Built for Helix: a spreadsheet that feels like one, made for scientific tables.

R, for the statistics

The reference for statistics, running inside the app through webR (R compiled to WebAssembly).

Plotly, for the graphs

Crisp, interactive graphs from Plotly, exported ready for a paper.

Features

Tables, analyses, graphs

Each table holds its own analyses and graphs, which update as soon as the data change.

Tables

Six table types, each offering the analyses and graphs that fit its data.

  • Column, XY, Grouped, Multiple Variables, Contingency and Survival tables
  • A spreadsheet that feels like one: copy and paste with Excel or Numbers, fill down, find, undo and redo everything
  • Exclude a value from the analyses without deleting it
  • Transform or normalize (logarithms, percent of control, z-scores) into a table that follows its own
  • Notes and color tags, as in the Finder, to find your way in a large project

Analyses, guided

Assistants ask the right questions, pick the right test and can check normality for you. Every result comes from R.

  • Descriptive statistics, normality (Shapiro-Wilk) and outliers (ROUT, Grubbs)
  • Two groups: t tests (unpaired, Welch, paired, ratio, lognormal), Mann-Whitney, Wilcoxon, with effect sizes
  • More groups: one-way, Welch’s and repeated-measures ANOVA, mixed-effects model, Kruskal-Wallis, Friedman, with multiple comparisons (Tukey, Dunnett, Šidák, Games-Howell, Dunn…)
  • Grouped data: two-way ANOVA (ordinary, repeated measures, Scheirer-Ray-Hare) and multiple t tests
  • XY and variables: correlation, linear, nonlinear (dose-response, growth, decay…), logistic and multiple regression, standard curves, area under the curve
  • Survival: Kaplan-Meier, log-rank test, hazard ratios and Cox regression
  • Contingency: chi-square and Fisher’s exact test, relative risk, odds ratio, sensitivity and specificity

Clear results

Every analysis gets its own report, computed by R, the reference for statistics.

  • Always in sync: results update as soon as the data change
  • Every result opens with the data it analyzed, what it means in plain words and its assumptions checked
  • Results feed the graphs: significance stars, group letters and fitted curves come straight from your analyses

Graphs ready for a paper

The right graphs for each table, and figures you can send to a journal as they are.

  • Individual values, box and violin plots, bars, lines, scatter plots, histograms, dose-response curves, Kaplan-Meier survival curves, heatmaps, pie charts
  • Volcano plots that handle tens of thousands of genes
  • Log axes, 95% CI error bars, reference lines, and the number at risk under survival curves
  • Resize by dragging an axis or set exact sizes, white or transparent background
  • Your own palettes, kept on your Mac and ready in every project
  • Match a graph to another one: colors, points, error bars, text, size…
  • Export as PNG at 150, 300 or 600 dpi, SVG or JPG, or copy and paste anywhere

Free, open, private

Your data stay yours

Private

Helix works entirely on your Mac: your data never leave it, and there is no telemetry. Its only connections are a daily check on GitHub for a newer version, and the updates you choose to install.

Open source

Every line of code is public on GitHub, under the GPL-3.0 license. Free today, free for good.

Never locked in

A Helix project (.hlx) is plain JSON, documented in the open. Your data stay readable with or without Helix.

A real Mac app

Built with Tauri, not Electron: light and fast, with native menus and shortcuts, light and dark mode, and several projects open side by side, each in its own window.

Try it in one click

Help › Sample Project opens a ready-made project with many kinds of tables, analyses and graphs to explore.

Stays up to date

Helix asks before each update, installs it and relaunches with your projects. See what changed in the release notes.

Helix is designed and maintained by a scientist. Every analysis and graph is checked against R by an automated test suite, the code is reviewed and audited with the help of Claude Code, and the whole history is public on GitHub.
